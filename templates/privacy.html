Privacy Policy & Scripture Notices

Last Updated: October 13, 2026 • Package ID: ElroiTheCreator.HolyBible

1. Product Privacy Policy (WinGet Policy 1.5.1)

Holy Bible (வேதம்) is an offline-first bilingual desktop and web application. You can read all bundled scripture canons, search verses, use Presenter mode, take interactive quizzes, and follow reading plans without creating an account or providing personal information.

A. Data Processed & Purposes

  • Local Device Storage (Default): Reading progress (bible_read_chapters), bookmarks (bilingual_bible_bookmarks), verse highlights (bilingual_bible_highlights), custom reading plans (bible_custom_reading_plans), and display/voice preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Optional Email Authentication & Cloud Sync (Supabase): If you choose to sign in via Settings → Sign In with Email, your email address is used for passwordless magic-link authentication and to synchronize your reading history, bookmarks, highlights, custom reading plans, preferences (user_bible_sync), and optional daily reminder Web Push subscriptions (user_push_subscriptions) through Supabase, Inc. (Supabase Privacy Policy).
  • Text-to-Speech (TTS) Audio Synthesis: When you stream chapter audio or voice samples (/api/audio/stream), only the selected scripture verse text, language code (ta/en), voice name, and playback rate are sent to Microsoft Edge Neural TTS (edge-tts, Microsoft Privacy Statement) with Google Text-to-Speech (gTTS, Google Privacy Policy) as fallback. No personal identifiers or email addresses are transmitted with TTS requests.

B. Data Retention, Deletion & Self-Service Account Removal

  • Local data remains on your device until cleared or uninstalled. You can export a portable JSON copy at any time via Export Backup.
  • Cloud-synced records in Supabase (user_bible_sync and user_push_subscriptions) are retained only while you keep cloud sync active.
  • Instant Self-Service Cloud Deletion: Signed-in users can permanently delete all cloud-synced records and push subscriptions at any time using the Delete Cloud Data button in Settings (⚙️) → Cloud Sync or below, or by opening a deletion request at GitHub Issues.
